# Regulatory Escalation Workflow

## Trigger Conditions

- Confirmed sensitive data exposure
- Mass export threshold exceeded
- Evidence of external transmission

## Escalation Path

1. SOC validates anomaly
2. DBA containment executed
3. Legal notified
4. Data Protection Officer engaged
5. Regulatory notification initiated (within 72 hours)
6. Executive briefing prepared

## Compliance Impact

Demonstrates structured breach handling discipline.
