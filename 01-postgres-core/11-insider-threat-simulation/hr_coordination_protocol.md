# HR Coordination Protocol

## Objectives

- Preserve evidence integrity
- Avoid premature confrontation
- Protect whistleblower anonymity
- Maintain legal defensibility

## Procedure

1. Disable user access quietly
2. Preserve logs before rotation
3. Notify HR and Legal
4. Initiate formal internal investigation
5. Document chain of custody

## Risk Considerations

- Do not alert suspect prematurely
- Maintain confidentiality
- Avoid log tampering
