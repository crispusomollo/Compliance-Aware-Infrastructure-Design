Provide real-time compliance posture scoring based on:

- Encryption controls
- Audit logging
- Replication state
- Backup presence

- Privilege enforcement

Risk Level: LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH

- Immediate risk visibility
- Continuous compliance monitoring
- Audit-readiness validation
- Board-level reporting support
